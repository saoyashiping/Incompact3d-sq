# P0.2 Force-Buffer-to-RHS Audit

P0.2 adds `fibre_prod_main_hook_apply_force_buffer(rhs_x, rhs_y, rhs_z, force_buffer, status)` as the buffer-level production path.

The path validates a `fibre_prod_force_buffer_type` and then passes `force_buffer%fx`, `force_buffer%fy`, and `force_buffer%fz` to the RHS adapter. The adapter applies the explicit P0.2 scaling:

```fortran
rhs_x = rhs_x + lambda_fsi * penalty_beta * force_buffer%fx
rhs_y = rhs_y + lambda_fsi * penalty_beta * force_buffer%fy
rhs_z = rhs_z + lambda_fsi * penalty_beta * force_buffer%fz
```

This is not the removed uniform small-lambda contribution path. RHS increments are buffer-shaped and component-wise.
