# P0.2 Spreading Conservation Audit

The new `fibre_prod_force_buffer_rhs_path_check` performs the conservation micro-check:

1. Initializes a small structured grid.
2. Allocates a `fibre_prod_force_buffer_type`.
3. Calls `fibre_prod_spread_point_force` with a Lagrangian point force.
4. Calls `fibre_prod_force_buffer_total_force` and checks that the volume-integrated Eulerian force-density buffer matches the input Lagrangian force.
5. Routes the buffer through the main hook and checks that volume-integrated RHS equals `lambda_fsi * penalty_beta *` the integrated force buffer.

The check rejects uniform whole-domain RHS increments and also covers lambda=0 no-op and invalid-buffer blocking.
